What the IR office asked the AI to write 
Write a short summary of this table for the scheduling committee. The
program, student group, and scenario are shown above the table.

1. Start with what the numbers show. No interpretation yet.
2. Then give the most likely cause and one other possible cause.
3. Then give three ways to fix it. Each should address the cause, not
   just the symptom. At least one should change a policy or the course
   sequence rather than add a service. One should cost less than the
   obvious fix. For each, say what it assumes and name one number to
   watch next term to see if it worked.
4. Write one sentence that sums up the whole finding, in the form
   "... and ... but ... therefore ...".
5. Say plainly what this table does not tell us.

Use plain language a dean would use. Short sentences. Say "the numbers
show" for facts and "likely" or "probably" for your interpretation.
Do not use any number that is not in the table.
